        Distances are from the centre of the Earth. One lunar distance is the average distance to the Moon, 384,400 km. Sizes are a range because brightness alone can't tell a small shiny rock from a larger dark one. A close approach is not a collision: these are the distances each object will pass at. Any chance of an impact is watched separately by NASA's Sentry system. Select a name to see its orbit on JPL's website.
